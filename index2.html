Science Research Project

Recycling Nuclear Fuel More Efficiently

My project looks at how to get more usable energy out of spent nuclear fuel and cut down the waste that has to be stored.

The Problem

A reactor only uses a small part of its fuel before the fuel has to be removed. What comes out is not all waste. Most of it is still uranium, and some of it is plutonium that can be used as fuel again.

~95%Uranium
~3%Plutonium
~2%Fission products and other waste

Approximate figures for typical light-water reactor fuel. Add your own source here.

Research Question

How can the recycling of spent nuclear fuel be made more efficient, so that more of it is reused and less of it ends up as long-term waste?

What I Am Investigating

  • How current reprocessing methods separate usable material from waste
  • Where those methods lose efficiency or create extra waste
  • Which improvements look most promising, and why
